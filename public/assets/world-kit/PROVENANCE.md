# Tidewatch Harbour kit — revision 1

One world unit is one metre. All GLBs are static, Y-up, with local identity
transforms and ground-contact origins. Positive Z faces the front of buildings.
Place at scale 1. The character scale reference is the existing 1.82 m authored
human; its geometry is unchanged.

Nine meshes are adapted from the existing Kenney Pirate Kit files in this
repository. Source files were misnamed; the exact paths and SHA-256 values in
`manifest.json` identify what was actually reused. Materials, UV layout, origins,
scale and selected edge bevels were adapted. Source animations were removed from
the static chest and rowboat. The original files remain intact for compatibility.

Original source and licence: [Kenney Pirate Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/pirate-kit),
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), verified
7 October 2026. CC0 permits commercial use, modification and redistribution,
including inclusion in generated games. No external asset pack was downloaded.

The other fourteen meshes and all replacement materials/textures were authored
for this project by its Codex assistant. The project owner may use, modify and
redistribute this generated output. This statement does not relicense the rest
of the repository. Source: `tools/world-kit/author.py`.

The two 128 × 128 wood grain PNGs are original deterministic procedural textures
generated by that script. GLBs embed their required textures; no missing relative
image paths. Other surfaces use ordinary PBR colour factors: rough wood/canvas,
muted stone/foliage, restrained iron, and opaque amber lantern glass. Lanterns are
decorative props and do not add light entities.

Thumbnail PNGs are 256 × 192 renders of these exact GLBs through the existing
cached loader and the same neutral three-quarter framing as live Asset Browser
previews. Reproduce with `node tools/world-kit/thumbnails.mjs` against the local
editor after exporting the kit.

Practical dimensions: barrel 1 m high; crate .7 m; table .775 m; stool about .44 m;
wall/post 2.4 m; door clear opening about 1.0 × 2.1 m; shack 3.15 m at the ridge;
steps .18 m rise/.35 m tread; floor about 2 × 2 m; dock about 2 × 2 m and .65 m high;
palm 3.8 m. Exact exported dimensions and triangle counts are in `manifest.json`.

Collision remains the existing grid model. Stairs and raised platforms are visual
pieces; this kit does not add height-aware movement, mesh collision or interiors.
Use existing object definition/instance collision controls when authoring gameplay.
